Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Photo Enhancer & AI Filters (developed by G Interactive Studio) handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Our application is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information (such as your name, email, or contacts).

2. Photo Processing & Gallery Access

We respect your personal photos. The app uses the native Android Photo Picker, meaning we do not request or require full access to your device's gallery or storage. The app only accesses the specific photo you select to edit.

100% On-Device Processing: All image enhancements and AI filter applications are processed locally on your device's hardware. Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any external servers or cloud services.

3. In-App Purchases

We offer a lifetime premium access option. All transactions are securely processed through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or have access to your credit card details or financial information.

4. Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) to personalize ads and analyze ad performance. By using this app, you consent to the data collection policies of Google AdMob.

5. Data Security

Because your photos and edits never leave your device, they remain entirely under your control. We rely on the robust security measures provided by the Android operating system to keep your local data safe.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
